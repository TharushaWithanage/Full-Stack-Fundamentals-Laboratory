Kandy

Set amidst the lush central hills of the island, **Kandy** is Sri Lanka’s cultural and spiritual heartland, serving as the last royal capital of the ancient Sinhalese kingdom. Surrounded by mist-covered mountain ranges and organized around the serene Kandy Lake, this UNESCO World Heritage city is home to the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (*Sri Dalada Maligawa*), one of Buddhism's most venerated shrines. Known for its pleasant highland climate, rich traditional arts, and the world-famous Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya, Kandy seamlessly blends deep historical significance with stunning natural beauty.

Ella

Nestled in Sri Lanka’s central highlands at an elevation of over 1,000 meters, **Ella** is a charming, eco-friendly mountain village celebrated for its dramatic misty valleys, lush tea estates, and cool climate. A haven for hikers and nature lovers, Ella is world-famous for scenic landmarks such as the colonial-era Nine Arch Bridge, the rewarding viewpoints of Little Adam's Peak and Ella Rock, and cascading waterfalls like Ravana Falls. Reached by what is widely considered one of the most beautiful train journeys in the world, Ella offers a laid-back backpacker atmosphere filled with cozy cafes, tea factory tours, and breathtaking panoramic vistas.
